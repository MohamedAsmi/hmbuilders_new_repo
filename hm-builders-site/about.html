Who We Are

HM Builders & Material Supplies (Pvt) Ltd

We all work tirelessly with a dream of building our own houses from our own earnings and living happily with our families, but most of us are not able to achieve this dream. Our prime objective is to build your dream house for you with your own earnings.

HM Builders (Pvt) Ltd is an experienced construction company in Puttalam city. We have extended our services all around the island. We make your place strong & long standing with qualified & experienced technicians along with high quality building materials.

Our prime values are: high quality service, affordability & trustworthiness. We have hundreds of workers with long years of experience in building houses, commercial buildings and factories, and we've completed many government & private building construction projects around the country.

Our Story

From One Machine To A Registered Construction Company

The Founder & Chief Managing Director of our company is Mr. H.M.M Aathif, a qualified automobile technician who laid the foundation for our company in 2007 by starting a small scale business of cement block manufacturing with a single machine. He registered the business under the name HM Building Material Supplies in 2010.

He then began supplying building materials and undertook small scale contracts — specially to build houses for friends working abroad. In 2016 he established HM Builders & Material Supplies (Pvt) Ltd as a registered construction company under the Construction Industry Development Authority (CIDA) Sri Lanka. Our company is one of the main subsidiaries of HM Group.

Confidence and continuous effort are the secrets behind our growth from a cement block manufacturer to a registered construction company. Though new companies appear and disappear year on year, HM Builders has stood strong through every challenge in Puttalam city — providing hundreds of jobs, empowering subcontractors, and training the next generation of the construction workforce.
